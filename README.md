# KhonKaen Dynamics Co., Ltd. (KKD) 🚀

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **KhonKaen Dynamics Co., Ltd.**  
We engineering dynamic, reliable, and accessible hardware & software solutions — bringing Together Robotics, Industrial IoT, Computer Vision, and AI Automation to solve real-world problems.

---

### 🏢 About Us

Based in Khon Kaen, Thailand, **KhonKaen Dynamics** specializes in bridging physical engineering with intelligent software. From high-speed industrial automation to assistive technology and IoT monitoring, we build end-to-end hardware-software integrated platforms.

---

### 🧰 Technology & Capabilities

- **Robotics & Industrial Automation:** Kinematics, Parallel Delta Robots, PLC, SCADA, Custom Actuators
- **AI & Computer Vision:** OpenCV, YOLO, PyTesseract, Edge AI, Industrial Gauge Digitization
- **Embedded & IoT Hardware:** ESP32 / ESP32-S3, BLE, 4G LTE, LoRaWAN, Sensor Networks
- **Software & Mobile Applications:** Flutter (Cross-platform Mobile/Web), C++, Python, PHP, JS
- **Data & Dashboards:** Power BI, Cloud & Apps Script Dashboards, Real-time Sensor Logging
- **CAD & Mechanical Design:** CATIA V5, FreeCAD, Blender, 3D Prototyping

---

### 🚀 Core Projects & Solutions

* 🤖 **KKD-BOT** — High-Speed Delta Robot with Machine Vision | Cost-efficient, vision-guided industrial pick-and-place automation system.
* 📱 **D-GAUGE (Analog Gauge Digitization System)** — AI & Computer vision platform transforming traditional analog dial meters into digital logbook data via mobile QR scanning.
* 💡 **Jai Dee / Un Jai Lamp** — Assistive AI smart lamp combining OCR, Text-to-Speech, and motion control to empower the visually impaired.
* 🚛 **dumBLE** — Industrial safety solution utilizing ESP32-S3 and BLE-connected Flutter mobile apps to prevent dump truck body accidents.
* 💧 **Tam Nam** — Off-grid, solar-powered remote water level monitoring system leveraging LoRaWAN/4G LTE and automated dashboards.
